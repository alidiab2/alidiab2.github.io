About Coffer

Coffer is a fast, private file manager for iPhone and iPad with a real encrypted vault built in. Browse your documents, photos, videos, music, PDFs, and drives in one place — then protect private files with AES‑256 encryption.

Your files stay on your device and the storage you choose. No account. No tracking. Encryption keys never leave your device.
