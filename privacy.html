FileNest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

FileNest is a native iPhone file manager with an encrypted Vault. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled. FileNest is designed to keep your files on your device and in the storage locations you choose.

Files and folders

FileNest browses and manages files in its own storage and in the folders, drives, and servers you explicitly authorize through iOS. FileNest does not upload your files to any FileNest server and does not operate an account service. Your files stay on your device or on the storage you connect.

Photos

When you choose to import from Photos or save a file to Photos, FileNest accesses only the items involved in that action, on your device. FileNest does not read your photo library in the background.

Encrypted Vault and locked folders

The Vault and locked folders encrypt files on your device using AES‑256‑GCM. Encryption keys are derived from your password or stored in the iOS Keychain and never leave your device. FileNest cannot recover your password or decrypt your files for you.

Advertising

The free version of FileNest shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's SDK may collect and process device information and identifiers as described in Google's policies. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, FileNest presents a Google‑certified consent message (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings. Purchasing FileNest Pro removes all ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

FileNest Pro is a one‑time purchase processed by Apple. FileNest does not receive or store your payment details.

Data we do not collect

FileNest does not require an account, does not collect your name or email, and does not track you across other apps or websites. Apart from the advertising described above, FileNest does not send your personal data to FileNest‑operated servers.

Children

FileNest is not directed to children under 13. If ads are shown, they are configured according to Google's policies.

Your choices

You can revisit your advertising consent choice from Settings in the app. You can remove all ads by upgrading to FileNest Pro. You control which folders, drives, and servers FileNest can access, and you can remove that access at any time in the app.
