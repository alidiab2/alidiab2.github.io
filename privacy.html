Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Coffer does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your files stay on your device and the storage you choose. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising in this version.

Who we are

Coffer is a file manager and encrypted vault for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information. It applies to the Coffer app published on the Apple App Store.

Information we collect

None. Coffer has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or crash reports on our own servers. We have no servers that receive your data.

Your files

  • Your files, folders, and their contents remain on your device or on the storage you explicitly authorize (for example a USB drive, SD card, iCloud Drive, or a WebDAV server you connect).
  • Coffer does not upload your files to any server operated by the developer.
  • The app can only access its own storage and the folders and providers you authorize through the system file picker. iOS does not allow the app to browse all of your device's storage.

Encryption & the Vault

  • Coffer's Vault and locked folders use AES‑256 (AES‑GCM) encryption performed entirely on your device.
  • Your encryption keys are stored in the device Keychain and never leave your device. The developer cannot access, recover, or reset them.
  • If you delete the app or lose your device, encrypted content and its keys are gone and cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

Connections you make (WebDAV)

If you connect a WebDAV server, the server address and the credentials you enter are stored securely on your device and are used only to reach the server you chose. That data travels between your device and your server; it does not pass through the developer.

Device permissions

Coffer asks for certain permissions only to power features you use, and only for the item you choose:

  • Photos — to import photos and videos you select, and to save items you choose back to your library.
  • Camera — to scan paper documents into PDFs you keep in your files.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — to unlock your encrypted Vault or locked folders on this device.

Data obtained through these permissions is used locally for the stated purpose and is not transmitted to the developer.

Purchases

Coffer offers an optional one‑time in‑app purchase (Coffer Pro). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Coffer does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app is rated for general audiences.

Data sharing & sale

We do not share or sell your information, because we do not collect it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
